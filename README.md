# Kalima

Video courses, exams, and a teacher marketplace for Egyptian school students — from primary through secondary.

Kalima is for students, parents, teachers, teaching assistants, and learning centers who want one Arabic/English place to teach, buy access, and follow progress.

- Watch recorded lectures, download attachments, and turn in homework.
- Buy courses and packages, or redeem a teacher’s promo code into wallet points.
- Sit exams that can unlock the next lecture once you pass.
- Teachers (and their assistants) publish courses, lectures, and assessments.
- Parents follow a child’s lecture access and scores; centers run lecturers, students, and lesson schedules.

## Try it

The live site: [https://fekra-edu.vercel.app](https://fekra-edu.vercel.app)

## How it works

Students, parents, teachers, assistants, and centers sign in. Teachers publish video courses; students buy or redeem access, then watch lectures and complete exams and homework. Parents can follow a child’s progress. The same web app is packaged for Android.
